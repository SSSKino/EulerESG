- **Analysis reasoning**: The report discloses 'Percentage of electricity generated from renewable sources' with FY22: 55%, FY23: 59%, FY24: 61.5% (page 82). While this directly relates to the renewable share concept, the SASB metric TC-SI-130a.1 (3) requires 'percentage of energy it consumed that was renewable energy' calculated as renewable energy consumption divided by total energy consumption. The reported percentage is renewable electricity as a share of total electricity, not renewable energy as a share of total energy. The report also provides total energy consumed and renewable electricity consumed (in million kWh), but does not disclose total renewable energy consumption. Therefore, the disclosed value is a proxy and does not fully satisfy the metric definition, resulting in partial disclosure.
- **Recommended actions**:
  - Report the percentage of renewable energy consumption relative to total energy consumption, as defined by SASB TC-SI-130a.1 (3), including renewable fuels, on-site renewable generation, and purchased renewable energy with RECs/GOs.
  - If renewable electricity is the only renewable energy source, clearly state that the reported percentage pertains to electricity only and provide a separate renewable energy share of total energy or confirm that electricity comprises all energy consumption.
  - Provide the total renewable energy consumed (in GJ or kWh) alongside total energy consumed to enable direct calculation of the metric.
